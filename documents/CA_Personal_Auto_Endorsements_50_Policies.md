# MAPFRE INSURANCE — CALIFORNIA PERSONAL AUTO POLICY
# ENDORSEMENTS — STANDARD FORM LIBRARY AND PER-POLICY SCHEDULES (SYNTHETIC / TRAINING DATA)

This document contains (1) the standard text of each endorsement form used with Form PA300CA (01/25), and (2) the specific schedule of which endorsements attach to which policy, with policy-specific amounts.

An endorsement modifies the base policy **only for the policy(ies) to which it is attached**, as shown in the schedule for each form below. Where an endorsement conflicts with the base policy wording, the endorsement controls.

---

## PART 1 — STANDARD ENDORSEMENT FORM TEXT

### FORM CA-PP-NDE — NAMED DRIVER EXCLUSION ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the CALIFORNIA PERSONAL AUTO POLICY (Form PA300CA).

**SCHEDULE:** Excluded Driver(s) are listed in the per-policy schedule in Part 2 of this document.

1. The insurance afforded under Part A (Liability Coverage), Part B (Medical Payments Coverage), Part C (Uninsured Motorists Coverage), and Part D (Coverage For Damage To Your Auto) of this policy **does not apply** to any claim arising while an Excluded Driver named in the Schedule operates **any** motor vehicle, whether or not it is "your covered auto."
2. This exclusion applies regardless of whether the Excluded Driver had the express or implied permission of any person to operate the vehicle, and regardless of whether the Named Insured knew of that operation.
3. This endorsement does not remove the Excluded Driver as a "family member" or "insured" for purposes of coverage available to **other** insureds under the policy (e.g., a passenger's Medical Payments claim is unaffected).
4. This exclusion remains in effect until the Named Insured requests removal in writing and the Company issues an endorsement removing it.

---

### FORM CA-PP-RSE — TRANSPORTATION NETWORK COMPANY (TNC) COVERAGE ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement modifies insurance provided under the CALIFORNIA PERSONAL AUTO POLICY (Form PA300CA) in accordance with California Insurance Code §11580.24 and the California Public Utilities Commission's Transportation Network Company rules.

**DEFINITIONS — For purposes of this endorsement:**
- **"Period 1"** means the time during which the driver has logged into a "transportation network platform" and is available to receive a trip request, but is not yet engaged in a prearranged trip.
- **"Period 2"** means the time during which the driver has accepted a trip request and is en route to pick up a rider.
- **"Period 3"** means the time during which the driver is transporting a rider in the vehicle.

**COVERAGE MODIFICATION:**
1. Part A, Exclusion A.6 (public or livery conveyance / TNC exclusion), Part B, Exclusion 2, Part C, Exclusions B.2 and B.6, and Part D, Exclusion 1 **do not apply** to "your covered auto" while being used by an "insured" logged into a "transportation network platform" during Period 1, Period 2, or Period 3, **provided** this endorsement is shown as attached to the applicable vehicle in the Declarations.
2. **Limits of Liability during Period 1** are the lesser of the limits shown in the Declarations for this policy, or the minimum limits required by Cal. Ins. Code §11580.24(b) ($50,000/$100,000 Bodily Injury, $30,000 Property Damage), unless higher limits are shown in the Declarations.
3. **Limits of Liability during Period 2 and Period 3** are the limits shown in the Declarations, subject to any primary insurance maintained by the transportation network company, which this policy is excess over.
4. This endorsement applies **only** to a driver's use of the vehicle to provide prearranged transportation of riders through a TNC platform. It does **NOT** modify or buy back Part A Exclusion A.7 (delivery of newspapers, magazines, food, or other products for compensation), which continues to apply in full to any food-delivery, courier, or product-delivery use of the vehicle, whether or not arranged through an app-based platform. Food and product delivery driving requires a separate commercial or delivery-network endorsement, not provided under this form.
5. Coverage under this endorsement applies only while logged into the platform on the vehicle(s) and by the driver(s) shown in the Schedule.

### FORM CA-PP-RR — RENTAL REIMBURSEMENT SCHEDULE ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement modifies the Rental Reimbursement Coverage described in the Additional Coverages section of Form PA300CA.

**SCHEDULE:** Per-vehicle daily limit and policy maximum are shown in the per-policy schedule in Part 2 of this document, and supersede the base form's silence on specific dollar amounts.

All other terms, conditions, and exclusions of the Rental Reimbursement Coverage section of the base policy (including the requirement that both Comprehensive and Collision Coverage be purchased and that the loss be covered under one of those coverages) remain unchanged.

---

### FORM CA-PP-LLP — LOAN/LEASE PAYOFF COVERAGE ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement modifies the Loan/Lease Payoff Coverage described in the Additional Coverages section of Form PA300CA.

**SCHEDULE:** The applicable percentage cap of Actual Cash Value is shown in the per-policy schedule in Part 2 of this document.

The Company's payment under this coverage will not exceed the percentage shown in the Schedule, multiplied by the Actual Cash Value of "your covered auto" at the time of the total loss, in addition to amounts otherwise payable under Part D. All deductions described in the base form's Loan/Lease Payoff Coverage section (unpaid finance charges, excess mileage, extended warranty refunds, credit insurance refunds, past due payments, and collection/repossession expenses) continue to apply.

---

### FORM CA-PP-CE — CUSTOM EQUIPMENT SCHEDULE ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement modifies the Additional Custom Equipment Coverage described in the Additional Coverages section of Form PA300CA, and any provision of Part D that otherwise caps custom equipment loss at $1,000 — including Exclusion 6.a. (custom audio/electronic equipment) and Exclusion 12 (non-manufacturer furnishings or equipment that mechanically or structurally change the vehicle and alter performance or appearance, e.g., custom wheels, lift kits, light bars, non-standard paint).

**SCHEDULE:** The scheduled item(s) and scheduled limit of liability are shown in the per-policy schedule in Part 2 of this document.

Subject to the scheduled limit shown, the $1,000 cap in Part D, Exclusion 6.a. and/or Exclusion 12 (whichever would otherwise apply to the item) does not apply to the specific custom equipment item(s) listed in the Schedule. This endorsement applies only if the loss is covered under Comprehensive or Collision Coverage for the vehicle on which the equipment is installed.

---

### FORM CA-PP-WCD — WAIVER OF COLLISION DEDUCTIBLE (UNINSURED MOTORIST) ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement confirms that the Uninsured Motorist Waiver of Collision Deductible provision described in Part D of the base policy applies to the vehicle(s) shown in the per-policy schedule in Part 2 of this document. No separate additional premium schedule amount applies; this endorsement simply activates the provision, which is otherwise inactive unless indicated in the Declarations per the base form's own terms.

---

### FORM CA-PP-ANI — ADDITIONAL NAMED INSURED ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement adds the person(s) shown in the per-policy schedule in Part 2 of this document as an Additional Named Insured under this policy, with the same rights and duties as the Named Insured shown in the Declarations, except that only the first Named Insured shown in the Declarations may cancel or request changes to the policy unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Company.

---

### FORM CA-PP-LP — LIENHOLDER / LOSS PAYEE ENDORSEMENT

This endorsement adds the lienholder or loss payee shown in the per-policy schedule in Part 2 of this document as a Loss Payee under Part D of the policy, with the right to receive payment for a covered physical damage loss to the vehicle up to the amount of its interest, as its interest may appear at the time of loss.

---
